Grammar: Making Predictions

We use different forms to talk about the future and make predictions.

WILL

We use "will" for future predictions.

  • Structure: Subject + will + verb
  • Example: People will use more technology in the future.
  • Negative: Subject + will not + verb
  • Example: People will not stop using technology.

My Reflection

I also learned that feedback can help me improve. This unit was useful because I can use these topics to talk about my plans and goals.
