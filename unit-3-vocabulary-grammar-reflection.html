📚 Vocabulary

Word Definition Example Image
Prosperous Successful and doing well. I want to have a prosperous career.
Litter Trash left in a public place. People should not leave litter on the beach.
Upcycle To reuse something to make it useful again. I like to upcycle old clothes.
Therapeutic Something that helps you feel better or relaxed. Music can be therapeutic when I feel stressed.
Predict To say what you think will happen in the future. It is difficult to predict the future.
Gradually Slowly, little by little. My English is gradually improving.
Sharp growth A quick and big increase. There has been sharp growth in technology.
Continue To keep doing something. I want to continue studying English.
Estimate To make an approximate calculation. I estimate that I will finish tomorrow.
Remain unchanged To stay the same. Some traditions may remain unchanged.
Feedback Comments that help someone improve. My teacher gave me useful feedback.
Improve To become better. I want to improve my English.
Opinion An idea or belief about something. Everyone has a different opinion.
Achieve To successfully reach a goal. I want to achieve my goals.
Strength A good quality or ability. One of my strengths is being responsible.
Weakness Something you need to improve. Speaking is one of my weaknesses.
Motivate To make someone want to do something. My family motivates me to study.
Well-being Being healthy and feeling good. Exercise is good for my well-being.
Goal Something you want to achieve. My goal is to speak English better.
Constructive Helpful for improving something. My teacher gave me constructive feedback.

🧠 Grammar: Making Predictions

We use different forms to talk about the future and make predictions.

WILL

We use "will" for future predictions.

  • Structure: Subject + will + verb
  • Example: People will use more technology in the future.
  • Negative: Subject + will not + verb
  • Example: People will not stop using technology.
