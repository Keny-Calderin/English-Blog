🎓 Welcome to our English Level 12 Portfolio!

Hello everyone! We are Keny, Valentina, Angelica, and Dayana. This website was created as our final project for English Level 12 at Universidad Pontificia Bolivariana.

Our main goal with this portfolio is to showcase everything we have learned during this course in a dynamic and interactive way. Inside our content section, you will find: Advanced Vocabulary: New words we learned in each unit. Grammar Structures: Rules and examples of how to use the language properly. Personal Reflections: Our thoughts on our learning process, our strengths, and the areas we need to improve.

🚀 Why did we create this?

As university students, we know that mastering a second language is crucial for our future careers. Building this blog not only helped us review the class material but also allowed us to practice our digital skills.

We hope you enjoy exploring our project!
