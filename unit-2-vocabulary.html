📚 Vocabulary List

# Word Definition Example Sentence Image
1 Twice Two times I brush my teeth twice a day.
2 Pigeon A gray bird that lives in cities There are many pigeons in the park.
3 Banned Not allowed by a rule or law Smoking is banned in the restaurant.
4 Instead In the place of something else I didn't drink coffee, I drank tea instead.
5 Lawmakers People who make the laws of a country The lawmakers voted for a new law.
6 Glance To look at something quickly She glanced at her watch before leaving.
7 Kite A toy that flies in the wind on a long string The children are flying a kite on the beach.
8 Windy When there is a lot of wind It is very windy today, so wear a jacket.
9 Creep To move slowly and quietly The cat creeps toward the bird.
10 Apologize To say "I'm sorry" I want to apologize for being late.
11 Terraces Flat open areas outside a building or on a hill The hotel has beautiful terraces with a view of the sea.
12 Outlawed Made illegal by law That dangerous drug was outlawed many years ago.
13 Whistling Making a sound by blowing air through your lips My dad is whistling a song in the kitchen.
14 Forbidden Not permitted It is forbidden to swim in this lake.
15 Seem To look like or appear to be You seem tired today.
16 Backed Supported by someone Her idea was backed by the whole team.
17 Whisper To speak very quietly She whispered a secret in my ear.
18 Yell To speak very loudly Don't yell, the baby is sleeping.
19 Rush To do something or go somewhere very quickly I had to rush to catch the bus.
20 Switch To change from one thing to another Can you switch seats with me?
